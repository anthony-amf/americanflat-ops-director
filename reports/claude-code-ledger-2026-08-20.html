Where sessions ended up

last recorded state of each session

Almost nothing you spend goes on new text. 95% of the tokens are context being read back out of cache each turn, which is billed at a tenth of the normal rate — that ratio is why 157 million tokens came to $231 rather than several thousand.

Seven of 28 sessions stopped waiting on you, and five of those seven were the same problem: GitHub or BigQuery write permission the session did not have. Both were eventually granted. It is the single biggest recoverable cost in this ledger — those sessions did the work, then had nowhere to put it.
