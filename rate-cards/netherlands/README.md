The terms are the same on the 2026 sheet and the 2027 card: daily storage
measurement, max pallet height 1.50 m, consumables at cost + 15%, and a €6,222
monthly minimum that is not indexed. SSCC labels and packing specials are priced
on both. Drayage and the €17,500 implementation fee appear only in the 2025
Schedule 2.
