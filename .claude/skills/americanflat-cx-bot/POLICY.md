## NEVER say these to a customer

Internal levers only. Repeating them in a reply hands away negotiating room or
sounds cynical in a screenshot:

- The **$200/month per-agent discretionary budget** — the SOP says explicitly:
  *never mention an internal budget to a customer.*
- The **15–25% "keep it" discount range** — offer a specific figure, never the band.
- The **under-$50 keep-it threshold** — say "please keep it", never the rule or the number.
- That anger or review risk earns a better outcome (it is a real internal
  guideline; saying it out loud invites gaming).
- Restocking-fee or handling-fee **exceptions** you have chosen to waive.

## Channel first — it decides everything

- **Shopify / americanflat.com** — we own the whole return, refund and
  replacement process.
- **Any other marketplace** (Target, Michaels, Walmart, Wayfair, Macy's, Kohl's,
  Faire…) — send them to that marketplace's support.
- **Amazon** — wrong item shipped by Amazon: they contact Amazon support.
- **Non-Shopify order delayed** — the customer contacts the marketplace directly.

## Returns (Shopify)

- Window: **30 days from delivery**. Unopened, unused, original packaging,
  original packing slip included, or the return is refused.
- **Change of mind** within 30 days, item perfect: **20% restocking fee**.
- **Not our fault**: customer pays return postage, plus a **flat $5 return
  handling cost** deducted from the refund. Original and expedited shipping
  charges are never refunded. Alternative: offer a **15–25% keep-it discount**.
- **Order value over $100**: we provide the return label and deduct its cost
  from the refund.
- **Our fault** (damaged, defective, wrong item): get photos, replace it, no
  return shipping charged.
- **Good condition** return: send a label. **Destroyed or defective**: no label
  — the customer keeps or disposes of it.
- **Wrong item received**: under $50 they keep it; over $50 we send a label.
- Refunds processed **~7–10 business days** after we receive the items; funds
  available to the customer in **5–7 business days**.
- USPS via Endicia is the preferred return carrier. No printer → any FedEx store.
- **Non-returnable**: washed or used items, final-sale items.
- Always ask the reason for the return.

## Replacements

- Damaged or quality issue: ask for a detailed explanation **and photos**; do
  **not** take the item back; offer **replacement or store credit first**,
  refund only as a last resort.
- Delivered but not received: check tracking; no movement for **over 2 weeks**
  means it is probably lost. Replacement or store credit first.
- Lost-package claims must be reported **within 10 days** of the delivery mark.
- **After the 30-day window**: the agent's own call within their discretionary
  budget (updated 2026-08-17 — no longer "ask your manager"). Escalate only if
  it exceeds their remaining budget or the customer shows a pattern of claims.
- Accidental damage from misuse or improper care is **not** covered — but
  replacement or store credit is still allowed if it broke too easily, if
  damage was noticed late, or to save an at-risk customer.
- **Frameset missing pieces: ship the whole set** — individual frames will not match.
- Customer receives both original and replacement: send a label for one back,
  or invoice them through Shopify if they want to keep it.

## Product questions

- Exchanges allowed; a different item is fine **at the same price**.
- **All Americanflat products are Prop 65 compliant.**
- **Missing accessories** (hanging hardware, screws, brackets): issue a
  **partial refund** and link where to buy it — **we do not ship accessories**.
- Live inventory or ETA questions: Taylored Services portal / on-site contact,
  or the Ops receiving team.
- Out of stock on one channel: check Taylored, then check Amazon and point the
  customer there.
- Different prices across sites: explained by each channel's fees and shipping
  costs.

## Orders

- **No cancellations** — different time zones and facility processing. They
  return it after delivery.
- **We do not hold orders.** Better to order when it is back in stock.
- **No address changes after processing/shipping** — it has to come back to us.
- Add-ons ship separately with their own tracking.
- **No phone orders.** Purchases go through a sales channel.
- PO Boxes: only via Endicia.
- Unordered mystery Americanflat item with no traceable order: they keep it, no
  monetary exchange.
- Order not yet shipped and needed urgently: refund, or explain 5–7 business
  days; expediting is possible, but orders after 12:00 PM EST process the next
  business day.
- Tracking stuck on "Label Created" for 2+ weeks: confirm stock, offer a
  replacement, refund if they refuse.

## Response times

- **Respond within 12 hours.**
- **Full resolution within 24 hours**, or give the customer an ETA.
- No reply from the customer in 3–4 business days: follow up once, then close.

## Routing

- **Artists** offering artwork → assign in Zendesk to the Creative Director
  (Johnny Picardo).
- **Free product for a good review** (usually Amazon) → do not engage, no reply.
- **Samples**: none for ecommerce customers — suggest buying with the 30-day
  return. Wholesale follows the wholesale SOP.

## Logging — do not skip

- Refunds → "Replacement Orders" sheet, **CX Refunds** tab.
- Replacements → **Replacement Orders** tab.
- Return labels → **Return Label** tab (note scheduled pickups in column D).
- Discretionary spend → **CX Discretionary Spend Tracker**, one row per decision.

## Known tension — flag, do not improvise

On return shipping for a not-our-fault return, the two SOPs do not fully line
up: the Returns/Refunds page says the customer pays return postage plus a flat
$5 handling fee, while the Customer Service Policies page says that on orders
over $100 Americanflat supplies the label and deducts its cost. They can be
read together (we advance the label on higher-value orders and net it out of the
refund) but they are not written that way, so agents answer differently.

**Do not resolve this yourself in a customer reply.** Follow the customer's own
case, and raise the wording with the SOP owner so one version wins.
