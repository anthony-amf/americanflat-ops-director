# AF Slack Brief Template

Slack does not render CSS, so brand on Slack is about structure, voice, and tone. Follow the format below for any AF agent-generated Slack post.

---

## Structure

```
*[REPLACE] Title in bold, sentence case*
[REPLACE] one-line summary in normal text

---
*Section heading*
• Bullet one
• Bullet two
• Bullet three

*Section heading*
• Bullet one
• Bullet two

---
_Auto-generated [time] · Reply with questions._
```

## Voice rules

- Sentence case for titles. Not Title Case. Not ALL CAPS.
- One-line summary right under the title. No preamble. No "Hi team".
- Bullets are facts, not paragraphs. If a bullet runs more than two lines, break it.
- No emojis except for status markers:
  - ✅ done / resolved
  - ⚠️ blocker / needs attention
  - 🔴 urgent (use sparingly)
- Always sign off with a single italic line. Never with a corporate signature block.
