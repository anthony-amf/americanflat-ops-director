# Customer reply templates and the Americanflat voice

The three templates under "Established templates" are Americanflat's own wording,
from **CX: Customer Service** (Notion `19749ad7-8e83-4438-bfab-e60be99268ba`). That
page now sits under **Archived Pages**, so treat it as the house *voice* rather than
current *policy* — the policy always comes from `policies.md` and `returns-refunds.md`.

Everything under "Derived templates" was written to match that voice and to follow
current policy. They are drafting aids, not approved copy.

Zendesk **macros** are the first thing to reach for when one fits — they pre-fill
customer data and cover multiple scenarios. Read the macro carefully before sending;
several contain branches for different circumstances. Use these templates when no
macro fits or the rep wants something tailored.

---

## The voice

Warm, direct, and slightly formal. Short paragraphs. No corporate hedging, no
excessive apology, no exclamation-mark enthusiasm beyond the sign-off.

The shape of almost every reply:

1. **"Hi {FirstName},"**
2. Thank them for reaching out (and for their patience, if they've waited).
3. Apologize for the inconvenience — **once**, plainly, if something went wrong.
4. State what happens next, with real timeframes.
5. Ask for exactly what you still need.
6. Offer further help.
7. **"Have an amazing day."**

Recurring phrases worth reusing verbatim:

- "Thank you for contacting us, and for your patience."
- "Thank you for reaching out to us and we apologize for the inconvenience caused."
- "Please let us know if we can be of further assistance."
- "Once again, thank you for choosing Americanflat."
- "Have an amazing day."

Timeframes to state as written — never compressed to sound more helpful:

- Processing: **3–4 business days**
- Delivery: **5–7 business days after the order ships**
- Refund funds availability: **5–7 business days**
- Refund processing after we receive a return: **7–10 business days**

## Placeholder rules

Use `[square brackets]` for anything you don't actually know: `[order #]`,
`[refund amount]`, `[tracking number]`, `[product name]`. Never invent a value —
a fabricated order number or refund figure in a sent message is worse than a
visible blank the rep fills in.

## Established templates

### Late order / production delay

> Hi {Name},
>
> Thank you for contacting us, and for your patience.
>
> Unfortunately, we have a production delay and your order [order #] will not be ready
> to send until the end of the week, and you will receive it 5 to 7 business days after
> the order is shipped. If you are not able to wait for your products we will gladly
> refund your whole order. Let us know how you wish to proceed.
>
> Thank you — looking forward to hearing from you. Your patience and understanding are
> greatly appreciated.
>
> Have an amazing day!

### Reshipment / replacement confirmed

> Hi {Name},
>
> Thank you for the confirmation.
>
> Thank you for reaching out to us and we apologize for the inconvenience caused.
>
> The replacement order with the [product] will be processed and shipped as soon as
> possible. The processing time is 3–4 business days and you will receive this new order
> within 5–7 business days after it is shipped. You do not need to go through the hassle
> to return the original item and you can keep it as a gift from Americanflat. Please let
> us know if we can be of further assistance.
>
> Once again, thank you for choosing Americanflat.
>
> Have an amazing day.

### Puzzle issue (missing pieces / wrong puzzle in box)

The single most common contact reason.

> Hi {Name},
>
> Thank you for reaching out to us and we apologize for the inconvenience caused.
>
> We would like to offer you a new puzzle as a replacement. Would you like the same
> puzzle? Please let us know which puzzle you have. If you prefer a new design, please
> visit the following link, select and send us the name and picture of the puzzle you
> would like to get as a replacement.
>
> https://www.americanflat.com/collections/puzzles
>
> Please send us the following:
> Your full name
> Your order number or the marketplace where you purchased the product.
> Your shipping address to send you a replacement right away.
>
> Looking forward to hearing from you.
>
> Have an amazing day.

### Granting latitude — just outside the window

For day-32 cases where policy technically says no and judgment says yes. Lead with the
fix; mention the window without making it the point.

> Hi {Name},
>
> Thank you for reaching out, and I'm sorry the [product] arrived like that.
>
> I've had a look and I can get a replacement out to you — it'll ship within 3–4 business
> days and reach you 5–7 business days after that. You're a little past our usual 30-day
> window, but the frame arriving damaged is on us to sort out, so let's not worry about it.
>
> Could you send a quick photo when you get a chance? It helps me get the right replacement
> out and lets our product team catch whatever went wrong.
>
> Have an amazing day.

### A genuine no, delivered warmly

The "yes, and" structure: what we can do, the honest limit and its real reason, then the
path forward. Never cite "policy" as the reason.

> Hi {Name},
>
> Thank you for reaching out, and I'm sorry for the trouble.
>
> Let me see what I can do here. The package is already with the carrier, so we aren't able
> to redirect it at this point — once it's out of the warehouse it's genuinely out of our
> hands. What I can do is this: if it comes back to us, I'll get it straight out to the
> correct address, and I'll keep an eye on it so you don't have to chase us for an update.
>
> I know that's not the answer you were hoping for. Please let me know if there's anything
> else I can help with in the meantime.
>
> Have an amazing day.

## Never put these in a draft

- An invented order number, tracking number, refund amount, or date.
- A delivery promise faster than 3–4 days processing + 5–7 days shipping.
- A commitment that needs approval: post-30-day replacement, wholesale pricing, a
  specific deduction amount on a contested return.
- A reply to a review-for-free-product request. Policy is **no response at all**.
- An offer to ship accessories or hardware separately.
- An offer to cancel or hold an order.
