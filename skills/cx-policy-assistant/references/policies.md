## Artist inquiries

An artist asking to have their artwork featured on our products arrives through
Zendesk. **Assign the ticket to the Creative Director (Johnny Picardo).** CX does
not evaluate the submission or reply on the merits.

## Cancellations

**We do not offer cancellations.** Because of time-zone differences and facility
processing times, an order can't be pulled back once placed. The customer returns
the item after it's delivered.

Say this warmly but don't hedge — offering a cancellation we can't deliver creates
a worse second conversation.

## Checking order status

When handling an order-status question, always establish three things:

- the **marketplace** where it was purchased
- the **tracking number**
- the **order number**

**We do not hold orders.** If the product isn't available, the customer should
place the order when it is back in stock — once an order exists, it ships.

If an order was placed ~3 days ago, ask the customer to wait: orders normally take
**3–4 days to process**.

If an order is late and hasn't shipped, post in the CS Slack channel for an update
using the channel's standard format.

## Inventory stock-outs

**Non-Amazon sales channel:**

1. Check inventory levels in the Taylored Services portal.
2. Ask the Ops team (Receiving) for an ETA on the product.
3. If the Taylored portal shows nothing, check Amazon — if there's stock there,
   direct the customer to that listing.

Inventory questions in general go to the Taylored Services on-site employee.

## Missing parts

**Accessories** — hanging hardware for frames, screws for shelves, brackets for
mirrors:

- Issue a **partial refund**.
- Give the customer a link to where the part can be purchased.
- **We do not ship accessories.** Don't offer to.

**Framesets** — if a frameset is missing items, ship the **entire set**. Individual
replacement frames won't match the rest.

## Order adjustments

- An **add-on** to an existing order ships separately, with its own tracking number.
  Tell the customer to expect two shipments and two tracking numbers.
- **Address updates:** check with the Taylored Services on-site employee to confirm
  whether the address can still be changed. Don't promise it before that answer.
  Once an order is processed and shipped, the address cannot change at all — the
  item has to come back to us.

## Product

- We can **exchange** items, and can send a different item **at the same price**.
- All Americanflat products are **Prop 65 compliant**.
- Inventory questions → Taylored Services on-site employee.

## Pricing

If a customer asks why the same product costs different amounts on different
websites: it's due to **individual sales-channel fees and shipping costs**. This is
a straightforward explanation — give it plainly rather than apologising for it.

## Returns

### Inventory item

- Customer says it's **in good condition** → **send a return label.**
- Item is **destroyed or defective** → **no return label.** Instruct the customer
  to keep or dispose of the goods.
- **Wrong item received:** under **$50** → they keep it. Over **$50** → send a
  return label. Refund instructions in `returns-refunds.md`.

### Amazon.com

If a customer says Amazon shipped the wrong product and they want to return it,
**direct them to Amazon customer support.** Orders sold and shipped by Amazon
aren't visible to us.

### General

- **No printer:** the customer can take the label to any FedEx store to print it.
- **Duplicates** — customer received both the original *and* the replacement:
  - Issue a return label for the extra item, **or**
  - If they want to keep it, they can pay for it via a Shopify invoice — follow the
    wholesale SOP's **"100–299 process"** for invoicing.
  - If they request a **scheduled pickup**, note it in the Returns & Replacement
    file (Column D, "Return Label" tab).
- **Always ask the reason for the return.** Every time — it feeds quality data.

## Refunds

- If a customer is unhappy with the product, **offer a replacement first**; refund
  only as a last resort. Then follow their stated preference.
- **No reply?** Follow up **once** after 3–4 business days, then close the ticket.
  If they don't reply to that follow-up within 3–4 business days, close immediately.
- **Order not yet shipped and they need it now:** either refund, or explain it will
  arrive in 5–7 business days. If they're still upset and want it fast, we can
  **expedite** — but orders past **12:00 PM EST** are processed the next business day.
- Any money returned via Shopify must be recorded in the **Replacement Orders**
  sheet, tab **"CX Refunds"**.

## Replacements

- If we sent a replacement and the customer then reports the original arrived, send
  a return label so one comes back.
- **Problem reported after 30 days:** a replacement is still possible depending on
  the situation — *the SOP adds "consult your manager before acting. This is not a
  rep-level call."*
  ⚠ **SUPERSEDED (Anthony, 2026-08-17):** the manager gate no longer applies. A
  post-30-day replacement is **ordinary rep discretion** — if it fits the rep's $200
  monthly budget, they decide and act without checking. Escalate only if it exceeds
  the budget, the month is spent, or there's a pattern of repeat claims. The Notion
  page still carries the old wording; see *Standing overrides* in `sources.md`.
- **Pick & ship issues:**
  - AF.com orders missing pieces or damaged → get more info **and photos** before
    shipping a replacement or refunding.
  - **Lost in transit / tracking not moving** → add the replacement to the
    replacement log.
  - Frameset missing items → ship the whole set.

### Situations

**Order arrived damaged, or has a quality issue:**

1. Request a detailed explanation.
2. Request pictures to confirm.
3. **Do not accept the items back** — customer keeps or disposes of them.
4. Offer **replacement or store credit**. Refund is the last resort.

**Delivery confirmed but order never arrived:**

1. Check whether tracking is pending or hasn't moved in several days. No movement
   for **more than 2 weeks** means it was probably lost in transit.
2. Offer **replacement or store credit**. Refund is the last resort.

### Shopify orders specifically

- **Personal-reason return** within the 30-day window, item in perfect condition
  (too big, didn't like it, changed their mind): allowed with a **20% restocking fee**.
- **Return steps:** for order values over **$100**, Americanflat provides the return
  shipping label and **deducts the return shipping cost from the refund**. Once we
  receive the item and confirm good condition, the refund goes back to the original
  payment method. Funds take **5–7 business days** to become available.
  *(Note: the Refund Policy in `returns-refunds.md` states return postage is the
  customer's expense plus a flat $5 handling cost. See the conflict note in `sources.md`.)*
- Our replacement policy **does not cover accidental damage** from incorrect use or
  improper care.

We can offer replacement or store credit — and refund as a last resort — when:

- The item damaged easily (e.g. dropped from a low height).
- The item arrived damaged, was received more than 30 days ago, and the customer
  only just noticed.
- **The customer is angry.** Explicit policy: keeping the customer happy is cheaper
  than a bad review and the reputational hit.
- The order is **under $50** — the customer keeps it and you issue the replacement
  or refund.

## Response time

- **12 hours** — we must respond to the customer within 12 hours.
- **24 hours** — a full resolution must be given within 24 hours. If it can't be
  resolved in that window, give the customer an **ETA** for resolution.

## Review inquiries

If a customer asks for free products in exchange for good reviews (mostly on
Amazon): **no response is needed. Do not engage.** Don't decline politely, don't
explain — just don't reply.

## Samples

- **Ecommerce:** we do not offer or send samples to customers buying through
  standard ecommerce sites. Suggest they buy the item and return it within 30 days
  if they don't like it.
- **Wholesale:** follow the wholesale process (see `escalation.md`).

## Shipping issues

- **Pending order:** placed over 2 weeks ago, tracking still shows "Label Created" →
  confirm inventory is in stock, then offer a replacement. If the customer won't
  accept a replacement, you may refund.
- **Mystery item:** customer received an Americanflat item they didn't order and we
  can't locate an order number → **let them keep it.** They cannot return it for
  money.
- **Shipping address change:** cannot be changed after the order is processed and
  shipped. The item must be returned to us.
- **Order delay on a non-Shopify marketplace:** the customer should contact that
  marketplace directly.
- **USA PO Boxes:** we can only ship to PO Boxes using Endicia — see the Manual
  Shopify Order Process SOP.

## Phone orders

Americanflat does **not** accept standard orders over the phone. Customers must
purchase through one of our sales channels.

Phone calls themselves can run up to ~10 a day; aim to wrap a call in **under 5
minutes**.

| **24 hours** | Full-resolution SLA (or provide an ETA) |
